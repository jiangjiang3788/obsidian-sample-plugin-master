# Think OS Energy roadmap to 1.1.0

## Stable product rules

- Energy is Goal-bound and Direct Record based, not Template based.
- Quick capture points: `20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100`.
- Detailed capture: brain `0..100` + physical `0..100`.
- Detailed aggregate v1: rounded equal-weight arithmetic mean, while raw dimensions are retained.
- Missing is unknown, never zero/default/interpolated.
- Context/effects/patterns are runtime observations and are not written back as causal facts.
